Meet Vira.

Vira is your personal AI companion inside VIRALA. Tell her what’s going on, in your own words. She helps you understand what’s happening, shows you safe, practical things you can do, starting today, and builds a routine around what works for you.

For when you want personal support: someone to talk it through with, and help that grows with you.

What Vira does.

  1. 01

    Listens, in your own words

    No forms and no right vocabulary. Tell her about a hard night, a strange week or a question you can’t quite phrase.

  2. 02

    Remembers what you choose

    Tell her once. What you choose to share is carried forward, so you never start from zero, and you can change or delete it anytime.

  3. 03

    Connects the dots

    When something comes back, Vira helps you see the pattern, without guessing at causes or blaming everything on hormones.

  4. 04

    Helps you act

    Every conversation ends with safe, practical things you can do, starting today. You decide what to try.

  5. 05

    Grows with you

    The steps that help become Your Routine. Tell Vira how they went, and it adapts, only with your yes.

Her promise

Always clear that she’s an AI.
Never in place of your clinician.

Vira won’t diagnose or choose treatment, and she tells you when something is worth taking to a healthcare professional.
