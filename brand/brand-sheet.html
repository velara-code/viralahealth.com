Photography

  • Calm and real: soft sea, sky, mist, linen, windows, a notebook. Topic-matched in the Journal.
  • No faces, no hand-only close-ups, no fruit, no visible text or logos.
  • Graded softly towards Dusk (cooler, slightly desaturated); never filtered to look fake.
  • Text on a photo only over a Dusk vignette, with contrast measured from the rendered pixels.
  • Licences: Unsplash free licence (or owned), id and photographer recorded in review/PHOTO_RIGHTS_MANIFEST.md.

Rules

  • All text in ink on light grounds; porcelain on Dusk and night.
  • One accent: rose #A35866 on light grounds, its tint #E8A6B1 on Dusk and night.
  • Peach is never text: only inside the glow (hero card, Meet Vira, footer).
  • Alternate clearly between porcelain sections and deep Dusk sections; no pale in-between bands. Rose is the one accent. If it starts to look decorated, it is too much.
  • Every text pair WCAG AA (21 pairs computed in tokens.json).
