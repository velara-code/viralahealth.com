Vira’s symbol: a sparkle of our own

Vira uses the familiar AI-companion language, a sparkle, so people read “AI companion” at once. The points are built from the curved page forms of the VIRALA mark, so it sweeps softly and is never perfectly symmetric. There is at most one small companion spark, low on the left. Each concept is shown in one and two colours, on porcelain, mist and dark; at 16–96 px; as a chat avatar; on the Premium card; and in one listening frame. A is the one on the site.

Reference composition (schematic, for comparison only): Bloume’s “Meet Aiya” card uses one large, symmetric four-point star with two small stars clustered top right. VIRALA’s sparkle differs in every respect: curved, asymmetric page-shaped points with a sweep; one companion spark only, placed low on the left; and the VIRALA palette rather than a gradient.
