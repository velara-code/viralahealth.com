Vira’s symbol: three concepts

A small, living mark for wherever Vira speaks, on the website and in the app: chat avatar, Premium card, Vira’s header and a listening/thinking state. Each concept is original geometry from the VIRALA family (Fig #4E1A3D, peach #F6C2B0, clay #A13F28, porcelain #F7EDEA, lilac #ECE3F5) but reads as Vira, not the VIRALA logo. No face, robot, chat bubble, brain, heart or fruit, and nothing modelled on known AI marks. Concept C is the one chosen for the website (Meet Vira and the Premium card); A and B remain for comparison.
