The VIRALA app · in development

Your body, understood.

VIRALA is a companion app being made for early perimenopause, perimenopause and menopause, including when you’re not sure which stage you’re in. Share how you are in a quick check-in or in your own words. VIRALA will help you understand what’s happening, then suggest one or two safe, practical things you can do today.

Our approach

Science-backed understanding. Personal support that grows with you.

Checking in is the foundation. Understanding comes next, and it never stops there: every explanation will lead to something you can do today. Because VIRALA remembers what you choose to share, and what helped, its support grows more personal over time.

  1. 01

    Share it your way

    A quick check-in when you have a moment, or your own words when there’s more to say. You won’t need the right medical vocabulary.

  2. 02

    Understand, then act

    Every explanation will end in one or two concrete, safe things you can do today, chosen for where you are rather than generic advice.

  3. 03

    Support that learns what works

    VIRALA will remember what you choose to share and what helped, so its support grows more personal over time, and only with your yes.

What we’re building

Free to understand and act. More, when you want it.

VIRALA Free

The essentials

  • Quick Check-in A few taps to say how today is.
  • Understanding Clear, science-backed explanations, each ending in one or two safe things you can do today.
  • Your Story Charts over 7, 30 or 90 days that show what’s changing, and what’s helping.
  • The library Trusted answers for every topic, each with a clear “what can help” and the science behind it.

VIRALA Premium · planned

  • Vira

    Your personal AI companion

    Vira will respond to your quick check-ins and to what you write in your own words, help you understand it, then suggest one or two things to try today. She’ll always be clear that she’s an AI.

  • Your Story, deeper

    Your personal context

    Your own words alongside the charts, so you can see what’s changing and what’s actually helping.

  • Your Routine

    Where help lives over time

    Small, safe steps you choose. Say how they went (tried it, didn’t help, too much) and your routine adapts, only with your yes.

Everything here is in development. Features, pricing and timing will be shared when they’re ready.

Our promise

Never a diagnosis.
Always your decision.

VIRALA won’t diagnose conditions or choose treatments. It’s being made to help you understand what’s happening, take safe, practical steps, and know when something is worth taking to a healthcare professional.
