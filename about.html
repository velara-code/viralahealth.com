Made to understand you.

VIRALA is being built independently, as a more personal, science-backed way to understand your body through perimenopause and menopause.

What we believe

Understanding,
not tracking.

“A possible connection should never be dressed up as a confident diagnosis.”
  1. 01

    Understanding before anything else.

    Knowing what might be happening, and why, makes everything after it easier: the conversation with your doctor, the choices you make, the way you feel about an ordinary day.

  2. 02

    Evidence, with the nuance kept.

    Science-backed, to us, means showing where an explanation comes from, being honest about its limits, and remembering that general research can’t tell the whole story of one person.

  3. 03

    Your words, your choice.

    You shouldn’t need the right vocabulary to be understood. And what VIRALA works from should be what you’ve chosen to share, nothing more.

  4. 04

    Useful on a real day.

    Understanding is where it starts. Practical, optional support comes next, with healthcare decisions left to you and qualified professionals.

One chapter,
done well.

We begin with perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause, including the uncertainty of not knowing your stage. It’s a chapter that deserves far better than it usually gets, and we’d rather do it exceptionally well.

The VIRALA name leaves room for other chapters of life one day. That’s a possibility, not a plan or a promise.

Evidence is part
of the craft.

Journal articles are original educational writing, checked against the public-health sources they cite, including the NHS, the World Health Organization and the US National Institutes of Health. Each article lists its references with links and the date the publisher last reviewed them.

We keep general information separate from individual advice, avoid certainty the evidence doesn’t support, and say when something needs a healthcare professional. Healthcare arrangements differ between countries.

Good
questions.

Something unclear, or something we got wrong? Write to support@viralahealth.com. Please leave out sensitive health details.

Is VIRALA available now?

Not yet. VIRALA is in development and can’t be downloaded. There is no launch date yet; people on the email list will hear first.

Who is VIRALA for?

Women in perimenopause, menopause and postmenopause, including anyone who isn’t sure which stage they’re in. Only a clinician can assess that, and VIRALA won’t guess it.

What will be free?

Understanding: clear, science-backed explanations of what you’re noticing. A Premium tier is planned, with Vira, Your Story and Your Routine. Pricing will be shared when it’s ready.

Who is Vira?

Vira is the personal AI companion being made for VIRALA Premium. She’ll always be clear that she’s an AI, not a healthcare professional. There is no AI on this website.

What does “science-backed” mean here?

That explanations are grounded in relevant, trusted evidence, with their limits explained. Journal articles name and link their sources. It doesn’t mean the app in development has been clinically validated.

Can VIRALA diagnose a condition or choose a treatment?

No. VIRALA is for understanding and everyday self-care, not diagnosis or treatment. Please talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your own symptoms and care.

What will I get by email?

Occasional updates as VIRALA takes shape, and the first word when the app is ready. We only ask for your email address, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

How can I contact VIRALA?

Email support@viralahealth.com with questions, corrections, accessibility feedback or privacy requests. Please don’t send medical records or urgent medical questions; this inbox isn’t a healthcare or emergency service.
